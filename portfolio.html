Situation

When I took over as Head of Growth at Untitled in mid-2025, the business was pacing at only $660K against a $1.1M bookings goal—a $440K gap. The core issue was declining Google Ads performance due to match type changes that broke our primary acquisition channel, plus external market headwinds from tariff uncertainty affecting our ecommerce customer base.

Task

Close as much of the $440K gap as possible in one quarter (Q4) with only $40K in discretionary budget, operating as a solo marketer reporting directly to the board.

Action

  • Fixed the revenue engine first: Brute force negative keyword targeting in Google Ads to filter irrelevant traffic from match type changes. Daily monitoring and weekly optimization to restore CPL and volume.
  • Tested new channels strategically: Ran 30-day experiments at $100/day each on Reddit and LinkedIn with clear go/no-go criteria. Cut Reddit after 30 days (engagement but no conversion). Kept LinkedIn for intent-based retargeting.
  • Scaled cold email outbound: Brought in a freelancer for execution, drafted messaging and targeting. Started at 3k emails/day, scaled to 5k/day. Consistently booked 2–3 meetings per week with 50% conversion to opportunity.
  • Optimized existing pipeline: Fixed sign-up flow friction and conversion infrastructure to get more value from existing traffic.

Result

Closed the year at $1.05M in bookings—95% of target, missing by less than $50K. Google Ads recovery and cold email became the primary drivers.

Key Learning

Fix fundamentals before chasing new channels. Test fast with clear criteria. Don't be afraid to cut what isn't working.

Situation

At Unearth, we were positioned as a “construction project management platform” in a crowded market. Four problems were compounding: construction customers suffered from “Perfect Project Syndrome” and moved too slow for our growth trajectory; we were finding early traction in industries outside construction (utilities, municipalities); our own customers didn’t see us as construction software—they valued our mapping and GIS capabilities; and the construction PM keyword category had limited lead gen volume.

Task

Rebrand and reposition Unearth as a “Simple GIS” platform in 4–6 months, opening new markets beyond construction while executing a full demand gen strategy with a team of two. Needed to identify the right niche, execute the rebrand, and deliver tangible lead gen results.

Action

  • Website redesign & rebrand: Led a ground-up redesign—new information architecture focused on platform, solutions, and industries (not features). Customer-focused copy breaking down preconceptions of GIS. Identified 100+ keyword targets. Managed 2 hand-picked freelancers (1 designer at 10 hrs/week, 1 web developer full-time for ~3 months) while owning all IA, SEO, and copy in-house.
  • Content creation: Weekly blog posting to capture long-tail GIS search traffic. Long-form thought leadership (white papers on cross-bore detection, field data collection, digital transformation for utilities) gated to enroll leads in drip campaigns. Ran PR campaign around Series A announcement with embargoed pitches to industry publications.
  • 2 GIS microsites: Built a “What is GIS” educational microsite (5 in-depth pages) to capture high-volume basics keywords and reinforce our “GIS for non-GIS professionals” positioning. Built a GIS Data Hub with 1 national landing page and 50 individual state pages to capture geo-specific GIS searches.
  • Promotion: PPC lead gen across 5 keyword segments (drones, online forms, GIS, online GIS, utilities) with nofollow/noindex landing pages. Cold outreach campaigns to 5,000+ contacts across 4 segments (drone operators, utilities, construction, municipalities) using NAICS-code-based targeting.

Result

SEMRush organic traffic grew from 483 to 14,600 monthly visitors (~2,900% increase). Ranked for 9,200 keywords with $19.5K estimated traffic cost value. Google Analytics YoY: 160% user growth, 459% increase in goal completions, 127% improvement in conversion rate (3.22% to 7.31%). Grew competitive keyword market share from 0.05% to 10.83%—closing in on Esri’s ArcGIS at 23%. Broadened addressable market to utilities, municipalities, and infrastructure firms. Set the foundation for Procore’s eventual acquisition.

Key Learning

Content and SEO outperformed expectations—beating Esri for our target keywords was far beyond what I expected. Lead gen worked, but we were ahead of product readiness for a transactional model. Good positioning isn’t about what your product can do—it’s about what makes you different in a way that opens a bigger market.

Situation

At Showdigs (property showing platform), I joined as Marketing Director when the company operated in 5 active cities. The business model required geographic expansion to achieve network effects and scale economies.

Task

Scale from 5 to 20 active cities in one year while maintaining consistent quality, brand standards, and unit economics across all markets. Manage $1M annual marketing budget and grow the team from 2 to 6 people.

Action

  • Built a repeatable launch playbook: Templated campaigns, landing pages, and launch sequences that could be customized per market. Documented timelines, stakeholder coordination, and success metrics.
  • Multi-market campaign management: Developed geo-targeted paid search and social campaigns. Used Webflow CMS for market-specific landing pages at scale. Integrated marketing automation for location-based nurturing.
  • Cross-functional coordination: Timed marketing launches with operations team readiness. Collaborated with sales on lead handoff processes. Coordinated with CS to track market-specific performance.
  • Team building: Grew from 2 to 6 people, hiring channel specialists with clear role ownership. Built culture of experimentation, celebrating wins, and protecting team from burnout.

Result

Scaled to 20 active cities within 12 months. Achieved target CAC within 3 months of launch in most markets. Built reusable infrastructure that reduced launch time by 60%. 30% improvement in overall marketing ROI. Zero voluntary team turnover over a 2-year period.

Key Learning

Scaling isn’t about doing more—it’s about building systems that work without you. Document everything once, reuse it everywhere. Retention starts with hiring the right people and giving them clarity, autonomy, and growth opportunities.

Situation

I joined Unearth as the 6th employee and ran marketing for ~4.5 years, growing the team from just me to 6 marketers across growth, design, sales enablement, and product marketing. When I started, there was no inbound marketing infrastructure—no automation, no content library, no SEO presence. The company was fully dependent on outbound sales and conference leads.

Task

Build a demand generation function from scratch that could generate consistent, qualified inbound leads and reduce CAC compared to outbound-only sales. Scale it to support a company through a major repositioning, Series A fundraise, and rapid growth.

Action

  • Marketing automation & infrastructure: Implemented HubSpot for lead scoring, routing, and nurture sequences. Built the entire marketing tech stack from zero—analytics, attribution, CRM integration, email automation.
  • Content & thought leadership: Created a library of technical content targeting civil engineers, surveyors, and infrastructure professionals. Published white papers (cross-bore detection, field data collection, digital transformation for utilities), weekly blog posts, and gated content to feed drip campaigns.
  • SEO & microsites: Keyword-targeted content strategy that grew to 9,200+ ranked keywords. Built educational microsites (“What is GIS” with 5 in-depth pages, GIS Data Hub with 50 state-specific pages) to capture high-volume search traffic.
  • Paid acquisition & outreach: Ran Google Ads targeting bottom-of-funnel keywords across multiple verticals (GIS, drones, utilities, online forms). Built cold outreach campaigns to 5,000+ contacts using NAICS-code-based targeting across 4 industry segments.
  • Conversion optimization: A/B tested landing pages, CTAs, and forms. Improved cold email from 0% to 12% demo booking rate. Doubled conversion rate from 3.22% to 7.31% through funnel optimization.

Result

750% increase in inbound leads over 4 years. 334% YoY organic traffic growth. 50% CAC reduction via automation and improved nurturing. 459% increase in goal completions YoY during the GIS repositioning period. Built a team of 6 and a predictable, scalable pipeline that reduced the sales team’s dependency on outbound and supported the company through repositioning, Series A, and growth to acquisition by Procore.

Key Learning

Building demand gen from scratch requires patience and discipline. SEO takes months to compound, but becomes your most efficient channel. The early wins came from paid acquisition, but the long-term value came from organic. Invest in both fast results (paid) and compounding returns (SEO/content).

Situation

As a hiring manager, I repeatedly saw candidates ghost, drop out of funnels, or accept other offers because hiring processes were slow and impersonal. I wanted to build a product that kept candidates engaged throughout hiring using conversational AI.

Task

Take an idea from concept to market—build product, acquire users, validate product-market fit, and establish business viability as a solo founder with limited capital.

Action

  • Product development: Designed conversational AI workflows for candidate engagement. Built MVP with no-code/low-code tools to validate before custom development. Iterated based on user feedback.
  • Customer acquisition: Targeted small-to-midsize companies struggling with candidate drop-off. Acquired users through direct outreach, content marketing, and community engagement.
  • Product-market fit testing: Ran structured user interviews. Measured engagement metrics: response rates, drop-off reduction, time-to-hire. Achieved 30% improvement in self-sign-up conversion after streamlining onboarding.
  • Honest evaluation: Recognized that companies didn’t perceive candidate experience as urgent enough to pay for. Made the difficult decision to sunset and pursue more sustainable opportunities.

Result

Acquired 100+ beta users. Proved AI engagement could reduce candidate drop-off. Gained deep insight: hiring teams valued faster hiring more than better candidate experience. Ultimately shut down after recognizing the business model challenge.

Key Learning

Founding teaches you to validate willingness-to-pay early, not just product utility. The hardest part isn’t building product—it’s finding customers who will pay enough to sustain the business. Persistence becomes stubbornness without market signal.

Situation

Top Nutrition Coaching needed a 300% revenue increase over 12 months to reach “default alive” status. They were a lean team (4 FTEs + contractors) with limited budget, primarily relying on Google Ads and organic search for acquisition.

Task

Evaluate 14+ potential growth initiatives and create a clear prioritization framework that would help them focus limited resources on the highest-impact opportunities.

Action

  • Built ICE Framework: Scored each initiative on Impact (1–10), Confidence (1–10), and Ease (1–10) to force data-driven prioritization across 14+ competing initiatives.
  • Multi-channel revenue calculator: Modeled P&L impact of each initiative with industry benchmarks and TNC’s historical data. Every initiative quantified, not just estimated.
  • Identified leverage points: Top-scoring initiatives included optimizing first session show-up rate (automated SMS/email), expanding Google Ads keywords, reducing lead form complexity, and launching multi-channel paid social.
  • Connected to business outcomes: Structured everything around net income impact, not just revenue. “Revenue is a vanity metric. Net income is what keeps you alive.”

Result

Delivered a prioritized roadmap with clear action items, ownership, and measurement plan. The revenue calculator showed that even small improvements in show-up rate and form conversion would compound across volume. TNC advanced me in their hiring process and noted the work “went well beyond the assignment requirements.”

Key Learning

Prioritization frameworks are only useful when backed by quantified impact. Scoring initiatives without modeling their actual P&L contribution is just guessing. Build the model, then prioritize.
