Situation

When I took over as Head of Growth at Untitled in mid-2025, the business was tracking 40% below its annual bookings target: a $500K gap. The core issue was declining Google Ads performance from match type changes that broke our primary acquisition channel, plus external market headwinds from tariff uncertainty affecting our ecommerce customer base.

Task

Recover as much of the $500K gap as possible over the remaining six months with only $40K in discretionary budget, operating as a solo marketer reporting directly to the board.

Action

  • Fixed the revenue engine first: Brute force negative keyword targeting in Google Ads to filter irrelevant traffic from match type changes. Daily monitoring and weekly optimization to restore CPL and volume.
  • Tested new channels strategically: Ran 30-day experiments at $100/day each on Reddit and LinkedIn with clear go/no-go criteria. Cut Reddit after 30 days (engagement but no conversion). Kept LinkedIn for intent-based retargeting.
  • Scaled cold email outbound: Brought in a freelancer for execution, drafted messaging and targeting. Started at 3k emails/day, scaled to 5k/day. Consistently booked 2–3 meetings per week with 50% conversion to opportunity.
  • Optimized existing pipeline: Fixed sign-up flow friction and conversion infrastructure to get more value from existing traffic.

Result

Recovered $490K of the $500K gap, closing the year just under target. The bulk of the recovery came in Q3 and Q4. Google Ads restoration and cold email became the highest-leverage channels.

Key Learning

Fix fundamentals before chasing new channels. Test fast with clear criteria. Don’t be afraid to cut what isn’t working.

Situation

At Unearth, we were positioned as a “construction project management platform” in a crowded market. Four problems were compounding: construction customers suffered from “Perfect Project Syndrome” and moved too slow for our growth trajectory; we were finding early traction in industries outside construction (utilities, municipalities); our own customers didn’t see us as construction software, they valued our mapping and GIS capabilities; and the construction PM keyword category had limited lead gen volume.

Task

Rebrand and reposition Unearth as a “Simple GIS” platform in 4–6 months, opening new markets beyond construction while executing a full demand gen strategy with a team of two. Needed to identify the right niche, execute the rebrand, and deliver tangible lead gen results.

Action

  • Website redesign and rebrand: Led a ground-up redesign with new information architecture focused on platform, solutions, and industries (not features). Customer-focused copy breaking down preconceptions of GIS. Identified 100+ keyword targets. Managed 2 hand-picked freelancers (1 designer at 10 hrs/week, 1 web developer full-time for ~3 months) while owning all IA, SEO, and copy in-house.
  • Content creation: Weekly blog posting to capture long-tail GIS search traffic. Long-form thought leadership (white papers on cross-bore detection, field data collection, digital transformation for utilities) gated to enroll leads in drip campaigns. Ran PR campaign around Series A announcement with embargoed pitches to industry publications.
  • 2 GIS microsites: Built a “What is GIS” educational microsite (5 in-depth pages) to capture high-volume basics keywords and reinforce our “GIS for non-GIS professionals” positioning. Built a GIS Data Hub with 1 national landing page and 50 individual state pages to capture geo-specific GIS searches.
  • Promotion: PPC lead gen across 5 keyword segments (drones, online forms, GIS, online GIS, utilities) with nofollow/noindex landing pages. Cold outreach campaigns to 5,000+ contacts across 4 segments (drone operators, utilities, construction, municipalities) using NAICS-code-based targeting.

Result

SEMRush organic traffic grew from 483 to 14,600 monthly visitors (~2,900% increase). Ranked for 9,200 keywords with $19.5K estimated traffic cost value. Google Analytics YoY: 160% user growth, 459% increase in goal completions, 127% improvement in conversion rate (3.22% to 7.31%). Grew competitive keyword market share from 0.05% to 10.83%, closing in on Esri’s ArcGIS at 23%. Broadened addressable market to utilities, municipalities, and infrastructure firms. Set the foundation for Procore’s eventual acquisition.

Key Learning

Content and SEO outperformed expectations; beating Esri for our target keywords was far beyond what I expected. Lead gen worked, but we were ahead of product readiness for a transactional model. Good positioning isn’t about what your product can do; it’s about what makes you different in a way that opens a bigger market.

Situation

At Showdigs, I joined as Marketing Director when the company operated in 5 cities. The business had two distinct GTM tracks running simultaneously: expanding a two-sided marketplace (property managers listing properties, showing agents fulfilling appointments) into new geographies, and rolling out a software platform to property management companies that wanted to run their own showing operations.

Task

Scale marketplace operations from 5 to 20 cities while launching the software product to a national audience, all within 12 months. Manage a $1M annual marketing budget and grow the team from 2 to 6 people.

Action

  • Built a repeatable launch playbook: Templated campaigns, landing pages, and launch sequences that could be customized per market. Documented timelines, stakeholder coordination, and success metrics so each new city launch required less hands-on effort than the last.
  • Multi-market campaign management: Developed geo-targeted paid search and social campaigns. Used Webflow CMS for market-specific landing pages at scale. Integrated marketing automation for location-based nurturing across both the marketplace and software product audiences.
  • Cross-functional coordination: Timed marketing launches with operations team readiness for marketplace markets. Worked separately with product and sales on the software launch sequence, messaging, and lead handoff.
  • Team building: Grew from 2 to 6 people, hiring channel specialists with clear role ownership across paid, content, and lifecycle.

Result

Scaled marketplace to 20 active cities within 12 months and launched software platform nationally. Grew inbound pipeline 7.5x by tightening positioning, improving onboarding, and focusing on markets with real demand. Built reusable infrastructure that reduced new market ramp time by 60%. Secured $7.4M in Series A funding.

Key Learning

Scaling isn’t about doing more; it’s about building systems that work without you. Document everything once, reuse it everywhere. Running two distinct GTM motions simultaneously forced real clarity about which audiences, channels, and messages belonged to which product.

Situation

I joined Unearth as one of the first employees when the company was pre-revenue and had no marketing infrastructure: no CRM, no automation, no content library, no SEO presence. The team was small, the product was early, and the company was fully dependent on outbound sales and conference leads to generate any pipeline.

Task

Build the technical and content foundation for scalable growth: stand up CRM and marketing automation, establish an organic search presence from zero, and create the infrastructure that would support the company through its next phase of growth and fundraising.

Action

  • Marketing infrastructure: Integrated CRM and marketing automation tools to streamline lead generation and customer management. Built the full marketing tech stack from scratch: analytics, attribution, email automation, and lead routing. Established reporting that gave the sales team visibility into pipeline contribution by channel for the first time.
  • SEO and content strategy: Developed and executed an SEO strategy targeting technical buyers in the construction and field operations space. Published consistently to build topical authority, pursuing long-tail keyword opportunities where competition was low. Grew keyword rankings from zero to 10,000+ and organic traffic 334% YoY.
  • Website redesign: Redesigned the company website to improve user experience, reduce load times by 20%, and ensure mobile responsiveness. Rebuilt information architecture around customer use cases rather than features.
  • Thought leadership: Positioned Unearth as an industry thought leader through content targeting civil engineers, surveyors, and infrastructure professionals, establishing the SEO foundation that later scaled significantly during the GIS repositioning.

Result

334% YoY organic traffic growth. Keyword rankings grew from 0 to 10,000+. Website load times improved 20%. Built the CRM and automation infrastructure that became the demand gen foundation for the next three years. Played a key role in securing $3M in seed funding by establishing early traction and marketing infrastructure that gave investors confidence in the growth trajectory.

Key Learning

At early-stage companies, the highest-leverage marketing work isn't campaigns, it's infrastructure. Getting the foundation right, the tech stack, the data, the content strategy, creates compounding returns that campaigns alone never will.

Situation

As a hiring manager, I repeatedly saw candidates ghost, drop out of funnels, or accept other offers because hiring processes were slow and impersonal. I wanted to build a product that kept candidates engaged throughout hiring using conversational AI.

Task

Take an idea from concept to market: build product, acquire users, validate product-market fit, and establish business viability as a solo founder with limited capital.

Action

  • Product development: Designed conversational AI workflows for candidate engagement. Built MVP with no-code/low-code tools to validate before custom development. Iterated based on user feedback.
  • Customer acquisition: Targeted small-to-midsize companies struggling with candidate drop-off. Acquired users through direct outreach, content marketing, and community engagement.
  • Product-market fit testing: Ran structured user interviews. Measured engagement metrics: response rates, drop-off reduction, time-to-hire. Achieved 30% improvement in self-sign-up conversion after streamlining onboarding.
  • Honest evaluation: Recognized that companies didn’t perceive candidate experience as urgent enough to pay for. Made the difficult decision to sunset and pursue more sustainable opportunities.

Result

Acquired 100+ beta users. Proved AI engagement could reduce candidate drop-off. Gained the key insight that hiring teams valued faster hiring more than better candidate experience. Shut down after recognizing the business model challenge.

Key Learning

The sequencing matters more than the execution. Find a customer who has the problem before you build the solution. Confirm they’ll pay for it before you build the acquisition funnel. Then scale. I did those steps out of order, which meant I had users but not a business. Persistence becomes stubbornness without commercial signal.
