The Current Seller Journey Has Gaps

What's working

  • Doug DeMuro content engine drives awareness + trust
  • "5 minutes to get started" reduces perceived friction
  • Curated marketplace creates quality signal
  • Persistent "Sell" CTA + dedicated /sell-car/ page

What I'd build on

  • Hero image shows a cool car -not an auction
  • Leads with "it's free" -real value prop is "get maximum value"
  • C&B writes your listing for you -buried in step 2
  • Account creation gates the first click
  • Curation positioned as disclaimer, not prestige

03

Target the Biggest, Most Underserved Segment

Modified, well-maintained, or rare-spec modern cars (2000–2020)

Tuned WRX Clean E46 M3 Manual Accord V6 Modified Golf R Tasteful Miata Build

Why this segment

  • Highest volume -more of these than exotics or collectibles
  • Underserved -trade-ins undervalue mods, Craigslist attracts tire-kickers
  • Perfect C&B fit -enthusiast buyers bid competitively
  • Lower barrier -easier to acquire, builds funnel volume
"Nobody understands what my car is worth."

07

Learn Fast, Adjust Weekly

Weeks 1–2 Is the page converting?

Watch: Visit → submission rate

Test: Headlines, hero visual, CTA copy, form placement

If <5%: hero isn't working  |  If >10%: scale spend

Weeks 3–4 Right sellers?

Watch: Acceptance rate from C&B team

Test: Ad targeting, self-qualification on form

If <30%: wrong audience  |  If 30–50%: expected range

Weeks 5–8 Lifecycle working?

Watch: Accepted → listing completion rate

Test: Email cadence, motivational vs. tactical content, SMS

Weeks 9–12 Auctions performing?

Watch: Sale rate, avg price, seller engagement

Test: Pre-auction onboarding, engagement prompts

Red flags: Submissions up but acceptance down (wrong audience) · High drop-off at single step (friction) · Rising CPA without rising quality (creative fatigue)
08

What This Gets Right -and What to Watch

Benefits

  • Removes friction where it matters -account gate moved post-acceptance
  • Value prop sellers care about -max price, not "free"
  • Curation = prestige -aspiration, not disclaimer
  • Repeatable system -clone for exotics, classics, SUVs
  • Lifecycle prevents drop-off -acceptance → listing completion

Tradeoffs I'd Watch

  • More submissions = more review work -mitigate with pre-qualification
  • Exclusivity could alienate rejected sellers -mitigate with respectful rejection + alternatives
  • Paid LP could cannibalize organic -mitigate with UTM separation
  • Volume vs. quality tension -track acceptance rate by source, kill bad channels fast
